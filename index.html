About IPL 2026

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league featuring teams representing different cities across India.

Popular Teams

  • Chennai Super Kings (CSK)
  • Mumbai Indians (MI)
  • Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB)
  • Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR)
  • Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH)
  • Rajasthan Royals (RR)
  • Delhi Capitals (DC)
  • Punjab Kings (PBKS)
  • Gujarat Titans (GT)
  • Lucknow Super Giants (LSG)

Major Match Venues

  • Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai
  • M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru

  • Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad
